Privacy Policy – History Shorts Bot

Last updated: 5 October 2026

What this app does

History Shorts Bot is a private tool. It generates short videos and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. Nobody else uses it.

Data accessed

The app requests only the youtube.upload permission, so it can upload videos to the authorized channel. It does not read, collect or store any other data from your Google account.

Storage

The OAuth access token is stored only on the operator's own computer or server. It is used to upload videos and is never shared with third parties.

Third parties

Video content is generated with the Anthropic (Claude) and Stability AI APIs. No Google user data is sent to these services.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

For questions, use the support email listed on the app's Google consent screen.

Use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
